\section{Scope and Next Steps}
\label{sec:scope}

The evaluation characterizes predictive representations: every diagnostic is measured in the frozen V-JEPA embedding space or with a linear probe on it, and the interaction clips are candidates chosen by a proximity and relative-motion rule. Trajectory forecasting, a comparison with a calibrated social-force or agent-based model, and a check of the candidates against human judgements of who yields to whom lie outside the reported evaluation and are the next tests. The protocol tests generalization to held-out clips: a source video can contribute to both training and test, and no city is held out. The results cover Indian footage and one training run per configuration; video- and city-level splits, dense-traffic regions beyond India, and repeated runs are the natural extensions.
