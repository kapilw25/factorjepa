Agent Label Check

How to rate (about 45 to 60 minutes; you can stop and come back)

A computer program marked the road users (people, two-wheelers, auto-rickshaws, cars, buses, carts, animals) in 100 street scenes from Indian cities, and picked one pair of road users it thinks are interacting. For each scene, check its work with three questions. Work on your own and do not compare with other raters until everyone is done. Your answers save as you go, and only the study owner can see them.

1. How many road users did the boxes miss?
  • Count road users you can clearly see in the still frame that have no box. Ignore people far in the background who are only a few pixels tall.
2. How many boxes are wrong?
  • A box is wrong if it holds no road user (a sign, a shadow, a shop), or if one box covers two or more road users.
3. Watch the clip. Does one of the pair marked A and B give way to the other?
  • Giving way means it slows, stops, waits or swerves because of the other one. Pick Can't tell if the clip does not show it.

Keys: 1 to 4 for question 1, Q W E for question 2, A S D F for question 3, arrow keys to move. The scenes come from public videos; please do not share them.
