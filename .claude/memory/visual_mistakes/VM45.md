## VM45 — Enlarging centred tick labels on a symlog axis eats the gap between close neighbours (2026-11-27)

Context: FactorJEPA workshop drafts, Figure 3 (`overleaf/v4_AAAI_workshop/common/figs/forest_115k_col.png`). The owner picked 9 pt numbers; the 6 x-tick labels were re-rendered from 7.5 pt to 8.7 pt at the same centred anchors (`common/figs/edits/forest_numbers_9pt.py`).

- **Symptom (visual-audit C12 FAIL).** The ink gap between "-100" and "-10" fell from 43 px to 23 px at 600 dpi (0.32 em), narrower than a word space and only 1.8 times the gap inside a label.
- **Root cause.** On a symlog axis the decade ticks at the far end sit close together. Centred labels grow on both sides, so each 16% size increase eats into the narrowest gap twice.
- **Prevention.** After any tick font increase, measure every neighbouring ink gap. Each gap must be at least a word space (about 0.42 em) and clearly wider than the gaps inside a label. Otherwise nudge the pair apart by a few px (here 4 px each, gap 29 px) or ask the owner before dropping a label.
