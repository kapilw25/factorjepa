## VM46 — A white bar outline that meets the axis frame paints over the frame's innermost pixel column (2026-11-27)

Context: FactorJEPA Figure 3 (`overleaf/v4_AAAI_workshop/common/figs/forest_115k_col.png`). Bars are drawn with `edgecolor="white"` and run to the x limits.

- **Symptom (visual-audit FAIL 2).** The right frame was white at x=1810 for y 304-408 beside the 43.3x bar, and the left frame at x=772 for y 2090-2194 beside the -146.5x bar: 12 px wide instead of 13. An earlier audit had found and fixed only the 2 px notch in the top frame line.
- **Root cause.** Bars sit above the spines in z-order here, so their white outline paints the spine pixels they touch. Checking only the top frame line missed the side nicks.
- **Prevention.** Check every frame line row by row and column by column, not only the top. Fill nicks with the frame colour after confirming the column is black directly above and below (`common/figs/edits/forest_tick_gap_and_nicks.py`). When regenerating, draw spines above the bars or give bars no white edge.
