## VM39 — Raster-cropping one panel out of a multi-panel figure with PDF TEXT-BOX coordinates bled neighbour-panel ink and left a dead lead-in (2026-09-21)

Context: the AAAI-27 Student Abstract needed the 3-panel forest plot reduced to its Full-115k panel at column width. The panel was cut from a 600-dpi raster of `forest_plot_best_ci_paper.pdf` (a raster crop, because the kit rejects CropBox-hidden layers).

- **Symptom.** `forest_115k_col.png` shipped (crop v1-v2) with the 1B panel's `.9x`/`5x` value-label tails, a green bar end and its right spine inside the "115k" strip, and the `-100` tick label clipped to `100`; v3 then carried half-masked 0.6-pt y-tick nubs and a 13-pt white lead-in.
- **Root cause.** Crop edges were taken from `pdftotext -bbox` text boxes. A text box is not an ink extent: the 1B panel's `1.5x` value label runs to 383.8 pt, past its own spine at 378, and the `-100` label's box starts at 376 while its ink starts at 389.2. A white-out mask added to hide the bleed then cut through the wanted panel's OWN y-ticks (ink 389.6..393.1).
- **Fix.** Derive every crop edge from ink-run scans of the 600-dpi raster (first ink of the wanted panel, last ink of the label text), use no mask, and have the build script assert a zero pixel diff of each strip against the source raster (`figures/make_forest_115k.py`).
- **Prevention.** For every crop composite: zoom the seam at >= 6x AND run the source pixel diff before presenting. A docstring geometry claim is not evidence; measured ink is.
