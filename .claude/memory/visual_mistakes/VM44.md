## VM44 — One-sided 1-px faux bold on text baked at 1.4-2.4 pt fills letter holes and changes nothing at print size (2026-11-27)

Context: FactorJEPA workshop drafts, Figure 1 (`common/figs/denseworld_teaser.png`). The owner picked "Thicken in place" for the tag labels in panels (a) and (b) and the panel (c) legend. `src/utils/fig_teaser_embolden.py` OR-ed each label box's text alpha with a copy shifted 1 px right (weight 0.7).

- **Symptom (visual-audit FAIL).** 33-40% of the open letter gaps in (a) and (b) lost at least half their depth. The 1-px holes in 'e' and 'a' filled to 60-75% of stroke brightness, and the glyphs smeared to the right. The legend box mask also covered the key glyphs, so the colour bar got a grey 1-px column and the yellow keys got grey tails. The (a) tag pointers and the Bus border columns changed too. At print size (150 dpi page render) before and after looked the same.
- **Root cause.** The real defect is the type size: 1.4-2.4 pt as placed, already logged as VM40. Thicker strokes cannot rescue type below about 4 pt. The masks were boxes, not text pixels.
- **Prevention.** Measure the placed type size first. If it is under 9 pt, thickening is the wrong tool: regenerate the labels (VM40) or move their meaning into the caption, and say so in the option before the owner picks. If thickening is still wanted, mask text pixels only (no keys, pointers or borders), grow both sides by half a pixel, and re-run visual-audit before pushing.
